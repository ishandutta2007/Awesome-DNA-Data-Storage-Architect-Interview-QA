# Resources: Further Reading & Tools for DNA Data Storage Architects

## Landmark Papers
- Church, Gao & Kosuri (2012) — "Next-generation digital information storage in DNA" (Science) — foundational modern DNA storage demonstration paper
- Goldman et al. (2013) — "Towards practical, high-capacity, low-maintenance information storage in synthesized DNA" (Nature)
- Erlich & Zielinski (2017) — "DNA Fountain enables a robust and efficient storage architecture" (Science) — foundational fountain-code-based DNA storage paper
- Organick et al. (2018) — "Random access in large-scale DNA data storage" (Nature Biotechnology) — foundational random access architecture paper
- Grass et al. (2015) — silica encapsulation for long-term DNA storage stability

## Textbooks & References
- **"Introduction to Coding Theory"** or similar foundational coding theory texts — background for error-correcting code design
- **"Molecular Biology of the Cell"** (Alberts et al.) — foundational molecular biology reference (see also Synthetic Biology Engineer repo resources)
- Genomics sequencing technology reviews (search current literature for up-to-date platform comparisons given rapid technology evolution)

## Organizations
- **DNA Data Storage Alliance** — industry consortium focused on DNA storage standardization
- Academic research groups at institutions with established DNA storage research programs (search current literature for active groups)
